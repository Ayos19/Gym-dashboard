## Conclusion

The dashboard covers 1,998 customers and $75,530 in total revenue. The average subscription price is $38 and average monthly revenue is $2,098.

### Key Findings
- **Premium memberships lead.** Premium earns $38,035, more than double Standard ($16,802). Basic is the weakest tier at $7,449.
- **Discounts are not the main driver.** Customers paying full price bring in the most revenue ($33,840). Loyalty, Promo and Student discounts each add about $13K to $14K.
- **Younger customers spend the most.** The 12-19 group leads at $17,798, followed closely by 28-35. The 44-49 group is lowest at $10,053.
- **Personal training is almost evenly split.** 51.75% of customers train without a coach and 48.25% use personal training.
- **Sauna use drops with age.** Usage declines steadily from the youngest to the oldest age group.
- **Location affects revenue.** Fresno leads at $9,756 and Oakland is lowest at $6,092.
- **Revenue is unstable.** Quarter-over-quarter revenue swings sharply and drops steeply at the end of the period.

### Recommendations
1. Push Premium upgrades for Basic and Standard members.
2. Reduce reliance on discounts, since full-price customers already generate the most revenue.
3. Convert non-training members to personal training.
4. Study what works in Fresno and apply it to weaker locations like Oakland.
5. Track retention and seasonality to reduce revenue swings.
